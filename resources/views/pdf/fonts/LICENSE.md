# Fonts

`DejaVuSans.ttf` and `DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf` are DejaVu fonts (copied from `dompdf/dompdf`),
free to use and redistribute under the Bitstream Vera / DejaVu license:
https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html

They live here, inside the dompdf chroot (`config/dompdf.php`), so the certificate never loads
files from elsewhere.
